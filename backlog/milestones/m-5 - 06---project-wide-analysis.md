## Description

Outcome: rules that are only honest with a whole-project view (CC203 inheritance depth, CC502 cross-file duplication, CC703 circular dependencies) become available through a Rude project-level provider, or through a separate indexer distribution if a new decision chooses that route (decision-1). This milestone is blocked on Rude's API, not on effort: it starts only when the upstream capability exists or the indexer decision is taken, and its first task is that assessment. No per-file fakes are shipped under these codes in the meantime.
